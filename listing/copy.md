## Detailed description

> **Drop a research thread, pick it up next Tuesday.**
>
> Tab Piles saves a whole browser window — every tab, every favicon — into a named *pile* you can come back to days, weeks, or months later. No more 87-tab windows. No more "I'll get back to this" tabs that you never do.
>
> ### What it does
>
> - **Save the current window to a pile** — one keyboard shortcut, name it, done.
> - **Restore a pile to a fresh window** — every tab opens exactly where you left it.
> - **Tag, rename, archive** — your piles, your filing system. Soft cap of 10 active piles keeps you honest about what you're actually working on.
> - **Cmd+K to jump anywhere** — fuzzy-search across every pile and every tab, open it in one keystroke.
>
> ### Who it's for
>
> Researchers chasing one rabbit-hole while another waits. Writers with three drafts open. Developers context-switching between projects. Students working three classes deep. Anyone whose browser is their thinking space.
>
> ### How it's different
>
> - **Local-first by default.** Free tier doesn't contact any server, ever. Your piles live in your browser's local storage.
> - **No account required.** No login, no sign-up, no email collection in the free tier.
> - **No telemetry.** We don't know how you use it. That's intentional.
> - **Auto-archive at 30 days.** Piles you haven't touched in a month quietly move out of the way, but stay restorable.
>
> ### Pro (coming soon)
>
> - **Cloud sync** between your devices ($5/mo, $40/yr, or $79 lifetime)
> - **Per-tab notes** — annotate what each tab is for
> - **Snapshot search** — full-text search across the pages you've saved
>
> Pro is for people who want piles on their work laptop AND their home machine. The free tier is for everyone else, forever.
>
> ### Privacy
>
> Tab Piles stores all pile data in your browser's local IndexedDB. The free tier never contacts any server. Pro tier sync only goes between your own devices via a paid endpoint — never to third parties. No analytics, no telemetry, no ad networks. Open the side panel, hit Cmd+K, get on with your day.

## Privacy notice (required field, plain prose)

> Tab Piles stores all pile data (window titles, tab URLs, tags, notes) locally in your browser's IndexedDB. The free tier never sends data to any server. The Pro tier sync feature, when enabled by a paid user, sends pile data only to a Cloudflare Workers endpoint operated by Tab Piles for the purpose of syncing between that user's own devices. No data is sold or shared with third parties. No analytics or telemetry are collected from the extension.
